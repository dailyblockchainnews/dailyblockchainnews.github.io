Why Base funds don't appear in Coinbase after a bridge

Why don't my Base funds show in my Coinbase account? The bridge sends them to your wallet on Base; send them to Coinbase’s Base deposit address to credit your balance.

The Hashbeam Desk·Nov 27, 2026 · 23:13 UTC·4 min read

The Base bridge sends funds to your own wallet address on Base, not to your Coinbase exchange account. Coinbase holds your exchange balance for you, while your wallet is controlled by you, so the bridge can complete without changing the balance shown in Coinbase.

  • Your bridged funds should be at the wallet address that made the Ethereum deposit, viewed on Base.
  • To credit Coinbase, send the asset from that wallet to the Coinbase deposit address for the same asset on Base.
  • You need a little ETH on Base to pay the wallet’s transaction fee for that send.

Where the bridge sends your funds

A bridge deposit sends the selected asset from Ethereum to the same wallet address on Base. The Base network has its own balances, even when the address is identical to the one you use on Ethereum. The Base bridge background explains how bridging moves supported assets between Ethereum and Base; the key detail here is that the destination is your wallet address.

That address belongs to your self-custody wallet, such as MetaMask, rather than your Coinbase exchange account. A Coinbase exchange balance is an internal ledger entry: Coinbase updates it when it receives an on-chain deposit at the address it provides. Connecting a wallet to Coinbase, or using the same email for both services, does not combine their balances.

Take a simple case: you bridge 0.1 ETH from MetaMask on Ethereum. The bridge credits 0.1 ETH to that same MetaMask address on Base. MetaMask may still be showing Ethereum, so switch its selected network to Base to see the Base balance. Your Coinbase balance remains unchanged because no transaction has sent the ETH to Coinbase.

A common misunderstanding is to think “bridge complete” means the funds arrived at the exchange used to buy them. For instance, someone buys ETH on Coinbase, withdraws it to MetaMask, then bridges it to Base. The Coinbase purchase history can make Coinbase feel like the destination, but the bridge follows the wallet that signed the deposit. The funds are in MetaMask on Base, unless the bridge transaction shows a different destination address.

How to move funds into Coinbase

To move funds into Coinbase, get the deposit address for the asset on Base, then send the asset from your wallet on Base to that address. Coinbase accepts Base deposits for supported assets; the available network depends on the asset and what Coinbase offers for your account.

  1. In Coinbase, tap Receive and choose the asset you bridged.
  2. Select Base as the network. Coinbase will show the deposit address for that network; copy it.
  3. Open the wallet that received the bridge transfer, select Base, and choose the same asset to send.
  4. Paste the Coinbase address, check that the asset and network match, then review and confirm the send.

The send from your wallet is a separate Base transaction. It needs a little ETH on Base to pay gas, even when the asset you are sending is USDC. If the wallet holds the bridged USDC but no ETH on Base, the USDC can be visible yet the send cannot proceed until the wallet has enough Base ETH for the fee.

Network selection matters at both ends. Choose Base in Coinbase and send over Base from your wallet. Choosing Ethereum as the Coinbase deposit network means the receiving instructions are for Ethereum; moving Base funds there requires a transfer back to Ethereum first, with the extra bridge step, cost and wait that entails. Sending over a network that does not match Coinbase’s deposit instructions can keep the deposit from appearing, and recovery may not be available.

After you send, the Coinbase balance changes when Coinbase detects and credits the deposit. If the bridge says complete but you cannot find the funds in your wallet, check the bridge transaction’s destination address and view that address on Base. If the wallet balance is correct but Coinbase has not credited a send, check the send transaction status and confirm the asset and network against the Coinbase Receive details.

Related

How do I send crypto from Base to my Coinbase account?

Choose the asset’s Receive option in Coinbase, select Base, and copy the displayed address. From your wallet on Base, send that asset to the copied address and pay the Base gas fee with ETH. Confirm that Coinbase supports deposits of that asset on Base before sending.

Can I send funds straight from Coinbase to Base without bridging?

Yes, when Coinbase offers Base as the withdrawal network for that asset and your account. In Coinbase’s send flow, choose the asset, select Base as the network, and enter your wallet’s address on Base. That sends the funds directly on Base; a bridge is needed when moving assets between networks, such as Ethereum and Base.
