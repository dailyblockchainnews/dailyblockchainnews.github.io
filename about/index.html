About Hashbeam

Hashbeam reports news from across crypto: protocol upgrades and forks, consensus and client releases, network security incidents, exchanges and custody, stablecoins, regulation and market structure. We cover many projects and are not tied to any one chain, token or company.

We work from primary sources wherever they exist — specifications, release notes, code repositories, on-chain data and official filings — and we say so when a claim cannot be verified. Errors are corrected in the article itself, with a note on what changed. Hashbeam is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
