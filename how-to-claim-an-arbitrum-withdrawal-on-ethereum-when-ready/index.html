How to Claim an Arbitrum Withdrawal on Ethereum When Ready

How to claim an Arbitrum bridge withdrawal on Ethereum: wait until the bridge marks it claimable, submit the Ethereum transaction, and receive funds at the set recipient.

The Hashbeam Desk·Nov 19, 2026 · 04:54 UTC·4 min read

To claim a canonical Arbitrum One withdrawal, open its bridge history entry when it becomes claimable, then submit the claim on Ethereum.

  1. Find the withdrawal and wait until it is claimable

    The official Arbitrum bridge history shows when your withdrawal is ready for its Ethereum claim. Go to bridge.arbitrum.io, connect the wallet that initiated the withdrawal, open your profile’s Transactions tab, and search for the entry using your withdrawal transaction hash. The hash identifies the withdrawal; the wallet connection lets you find it in your history.

    Arbitrum One withdrawals have an approximately seven-day challenge period. During this time, the withdrawal message is waiting for the assertion containing it to be confirmed. The bridge’s countdown is an estimate of the wait; use the displayed claimable status to determine when to proceed. The bridge documents this history and claim process in its withdrawal quickstart.

    A fast route selected through a lifi bridge aggregator follows the underlying bridge’s delivery process, which may differ from the canonical Arbitrum withdrawal flow. A lifi bridge aggregator finds routes across supported bridges and decentralized exchanges to move or swap tokens between chains. For someone comparing a Base transfer with a native route, LI.FI routes from Base to Ethereum can change which bridge’s delivery steps apply. Confirm the route used before following canonical claim instructions.

  2. Submit the claim on Ethereum

    The claim submits the eligible withdrawal message for execution on Ethereum. Once the history entry shows claimable, switch your wallet to Ethereum and select Claim in the bridge. Review the transaction details, approve it in your wallet, and wait for the Ethereum transaction to confirm.

    Keep ETH in the connected wallet to pay Ethereum gas. A withdrawal can be ready to claim while the wallet still lacks enough ETH to submit the transaction. The claim is the parent-chain execution required to complete the withdrawal; the original Arbitrum transaction alone does not release the funds on Ethereum.

    At the protocol level, a confirmed assertion makes the message executable through the Outbox. The Outbox’s executeTransaction function executes an eligible withdrawal message on Ethereum using proof that the message belongs to the finalized assertion. The bridge interface handles the claim flow, but the Ethereum transaction still requires wallet approval and gas. Arbitrum describes this parent-chain execution in its withdrawal messaging documentation.

  3. The claim pays the recipient encoded in the withdrawal

    The recipient address was set when the withdrawal was initiated, and the claim releases funds to that address. Reconnecting a different wallet can change which account you use to view or submit a transaction; it does not rewrite the recipient encoded in the withdrawal message.

    Before approving Claim, compare the withdrawal entry with the transaction you initiated and confirm that the destination is the intended Ethereum address. If you initiated the withdrawal to a different address, the funds go there after execution. Adding another wallet connection at claim time cannot redirect them.

    This matters especially when you use more than one wallet. Say you initiated the withdrawal from Wallet A but later connect Wallet B to the bridge. Wallet B may help you navigate the interface, but the claim still executes the original message for its encoded recipient. Check that address before you sign, and make sure you can access it on Ethereum.

  4. Confirm the Ethereum receipt and recipient balance

    The withdrawal is complete when the claim transaction succeeds on Ethereum and the recipient has the funds. Wait for the wallet or bridge to show a successful Ethereum receipt, then check the recipient address’s balance for the withdrawn asset. For an ETH withdrawal, check its ETH balance; for a token withdrawal, check the relevant token balance.

    If the entry remains in a waiting state, continue waiting for the bridge to mark it claimable. If it is claimable but the transaction has not been submitted, check that the wallet is on Ethereum and has ETH for gas. After confirmation, the successful receipt and recipient balance are the checks that establish completion. Your next step is to use the received funds from the recipient wallet on Ethereum.
