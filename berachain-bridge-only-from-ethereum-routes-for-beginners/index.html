Berachain bridge only from Ethereum? Routes for beginners

The Hashbeam Desk·Nov 23, 2026 · 23:12 UTC·3 min read

The Berachain bridge does not only work from Ethereum: routes into Berachain can also begin on Arbitrum and Base. Cross-chain messaging and relayer networks connect chains without requiring a transfer through Ethereum. To test a route from the chain holding your funds, use the Berachain bridge to look for your source chain and token.

The Ethereum-only belief comes from older bridges that treated Ethereum mainnet as the default starting point. Today, the question is whether your particular asset has a route from your particular chain. A bridge to Berachain moves assets across chains so they can be used in its DeFi ecosystem.

Every proposed transfer has four parts:

  • The source chain holding your funds, such as Ethereum, Arbitrum or Base.
  • The token you hold on that chain.
  • Berachain as the destination chain.
  • The token you expect to receive on Berachain.

Those parts must match a supported route. A bridge may accept one token from Base while offering no route for another. Say ETH from Base returns a quote but USDC from Base does not: Base is a possible source for the first asset, while the second asset-and-chain pair needs another route. Tokens with the same ticker can also have different contracts or representations across chains. Select the token you actually hold and inspect the receiving asset before sending funds.

Take funds already on Arbitrum: a supported route can send them to Berachain without first moving them to Ethereum mainnet. The Berachain Foundation describes its bridge as accepting EVM source chains. Bungee can search routes from multiple starting chains; Stargate and Across Protocol are other examples of cross-chain services. Each still needs to serve the exact transfer you want.

LayerZero’s messaging system connects EVM and non-EVM chains, allowing bridge applications to coordinate transfers across networks. Relayer networks offer another approach to moving assets between chains. Neither a chain’s connection nor a provider’s name establishes support for every token pair.

Enter the source chain and token, choose Berachain and the desired receiving asset, then request a quote. A returned quote confirms that route is available at that moment. Read both ends: what leaves your source chain and what arrives on Berachain. If no quote appears, try another asset or route provider before concluding that the entire source chain is excluded.

  • Routes to Berachain can start on Ethereum, Arbitrum or Base.
  • Support depends on the exact chain and token pair.
  • A live quote confirms the route you intend to use.
