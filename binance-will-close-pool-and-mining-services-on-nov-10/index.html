Binance will close Pool and mining services on Nov. 10

Binance will end pool and cloud mining services Nov. 10, cutting off Stratum submissions and setting final reward calculations for eligible shares.

The Hashbeam Desk·Oct 14, 2026 · 00:02 UTC·3 min read

Binance will discontinue Binance Pool and its related mining services on Nov. 10, 2026, ending miners’ ability to submit shares through the exchange’s pool and stopping new rewards and service benefits. The Binance closure notice gives the cutoff as 07:00 UTC and says users should redirect their miners to another pool before then.

The closure covers Binance Pool, Pool Savings, Cloud Mining and Transaction Accelerator, along with Telegram hashrate alerts, email notifications and mining pool API services. Binance says the move is part of its “ongoing review of products and services”; its notice does not give a more specific reason. The Block’s report on the shutdown likewise describes the Nov. 10 cutoff and the affected products.

What changes when Binance disables its Stratum endpoints?

At 07:00 UTC, Binance says it will begin disconnecting mining connections and disable its Mining Pool Stratum URLs and mining endpoints. These endpoints are the connection path miners use to send work results, or shares, to the pool. Once Binance disables them, the pool will no longer accept, process or credit hashrate submissions or shares sent after the cutoff. The exchange says no new rewards, yield or other service benefits will accrue after that time.

The change is at Binance’s service layer: the notice specifies which pool connections and products will stop operating. It does not announce a change to a blockchain’s consensus rules. Miners who continue hashing will need to configure their equipment to connect to another pool if they want to keep submitting shares through a pool.

Which shares and balances remain eligible for payment?

Shares submitted before 07:00 UTC on Nov. 10 will be included in the final reward calculation under Binance Pool’s applicable rules. Shares submitted after the cutoff will not be credited. Binance says eligible outstanding rewards, balances, refunds and settlements, where applicable, will be processed after the shutdown, subject to product rules, account status, risk controls, compliance checks and applicable laws. The notice does not promise payment to every account or specify a universal payout date.

Users should review active Pool Savings positions and unsubscribe or redeem them before the service ends. Binance also asks users to ensure their accounts can receive any eligible remaining payments, including by completing identity verification where required. It gives 23:59 UTC on Nov. 10 as the deadline for necessary account steps to facilitate timely processing.

How long will mining records remain available?

Binance says links to Pool will be removed from its homepage on Nov. 10, but the Pool pages will remain accessible for 14 days, until 07:00 UTC on Nov. 24. During that window, users can view historical data, account records and payout status where available. The pages will then become permanently inaccessible, so users who need records should download or save them before the access period ends.

The shutdown therefore has two separate deadlines: miners must move their equipment before the Nov. 10 cutoff to avoid interruption, while users who need Binance Pool records have until Nov. 24 to retrieve them. Final rewards depend on shares submitted before the first deadline and the applicable product rules.
